About me

Theatre and performance artist with a background in acting, contemporary dance, puppetry, and performance research. My artistic practice explores the relationship between the body, voice, technology, and contemporary forms of storytelling. I am particularly interested in digital performance, interactive media, video art, and the use of emerging technologies as performative tools. Currently completing my BA in Theatre, with a focus on developing an interdisciplinary practice connecting performance and digital art.
